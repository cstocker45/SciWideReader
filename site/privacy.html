Privacy policy

WideReader Chrome extension · last updated 2026-11-04

WideReader does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data.

  • The extension runs entirely inside your browser. It makes no network requests and contains no analytics or tracking.
  • It stores only your own preferences (whether it is on for a given website, plus font, size and spacing settings) using Chrome's local extension storage on your device.
  • It reads the layout of the web page you are viewing only to decide which parts to hide or widen. Page content is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Permissions: storage (save your settings), activeTab and scripting (apply the layout to a page when you click the button or press Alt+W), and access to listed journal websites (apply the layout automatically there).

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
